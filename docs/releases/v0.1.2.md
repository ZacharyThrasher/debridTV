# DebridTV 0.1.2

## New in 0.1.2

- **No made-up content.** The app no longer fills an empty guide with demo channels.
  Until your library is on the air, a "Put your library on the air" page shows three
  steps (connect a source, scan, watch) and switches to your channels by itself as soon
  as the first one is ready.


## Fixed in 0.1.1

- **0.1.0 could open an older DebridTV instead of itself.** If an older copy was
  already running (for example `npm start` from a git checkout), DebridTV.exe saw
  "something is on port 7337" and just opened the browser on that old copy, so you got
  the old look, station breaks and none of the new features. Now it checks which
  version is running, closes an older copy, and starts itself. Your settings are shared,
  so nothing is lost.

# What's in DebridTV

The first release you can download and double-click. It turns the shows and films
you already have (Real-Debrid, TorBox, or folders on your PC) into TV channels with a
printed-style program guide, and only fetches video for the channel you're actually
watching.

## Get it

Download `DebridTV-v0.1.2-win-x64.zip`, unzip it, double-click `DebridTV.exe`. Your
browser opens the guide. Keep the small window open while you watch.

- Not code-signed yet: on first start click **More info → Run anyway**.
- Settings live in `%APPDATA%\DebridTV`. If you ran DebridTV from a git checkout
  before, the exe picks up the same channels, keys and history.
- Nothing else to install. The first start downloads ffmpeg in the background for
  compatibility mode (about 110 MB, checksum-checked); most files play before it finishes.

## What's in it

**Watching**
- Channels that keep a schedule around the clock, with a program guide, channel
  numbers, favorites, logos and a "what's on" banner.
- Skip back and ahead freely, jump to the previous or next program, and "Back to live".
- On Demand: browse your library and pick any episode or film, with Continue Watching.
- A full player: audio tracks, subtitles (with an easy subtitle style menu), picture
  shape, audio delay, playback stats, and remote, keyboard and touch controls.
- New: **Skip intro** and **Skip credits** buttons (from chapter titles), **Next
  episode**, a **sleep timer**, and **reminders** for programs in the guide. Auto-skip
  and autoplay stay off unless you turn them on.
- Station breaks, idents and rating cards are optional and off by default.

**Your library**
- Connect TorBox and Real-Debrid read-only, or add video folders. A gentle scan learns
  each file's running time from a few kilobytes, never whole files.
- Smarter show matching: one show despite typos, release names and alternate titles.
- Posters and descriptions from TMDB (optional, uses your own key on your PC).
- Library view of what's ready, refused or still missing, with "try failed files again".

**Channels**
- Channel editor: schedule slots, marathons, premieres, seasons, weights, kid channels,
  restricted hours, PIN locks, groups, specials and off-season channels.
- Music and trailer channels; seasonal presets.

**Adding things (off until you allow it)**
- Search and add from Torznab/Newznab indexers you type in, the Internet Archive, and
  new in this release: **Stremio addons** by pasting the addon's manifest link.
  Nothing is built in, and addon play links are never opened.

**Home network (off by default)**
- Pair a TV or phone on your Wi-Fi with a code; revoke any device.

**Look**
- The "Listings" design: a printed TV guide look, with themes and a TV mode for the
  big screen.

## Known limits

- Tested on GitHub's Windows Server machines; first run on a home PC is still to come.
- Windows exe only for now; macOS and Linux run from source (`npm start`).
- The ffmpeg download needs the internet once, on first start.
- The exe hasn't been signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns once.
