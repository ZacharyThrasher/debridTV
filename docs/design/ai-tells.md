# What makes a UI look AI-made, and what we do instead

People now spot AI-built interfaces by a handful of repeated defaults. One tell on its
own proves nothing. Several together read as "a model made this and nobody chose".
This list came from what designers and Anthropic's own guidance point out in October 2026
(sources at the bottom). Every screen gets checked against it before a design PR.

## Do not

**Type**

1. **Inter (or Roboto, Arial, system-ui) as the only face.** It is the most common
   default. We set everything in Archivo, a grotesque with a width axis. It is condensed
   for titles and guide cells, where it fits more of a title, and normal width for
   reading.
2. **Oversized serif headline as the house style.** This is the 2026 Opus default
   (Instrument Serif and friends). Titles are bold condensed sans, like a printed
   listings page.
3. **One italic accent word in a headline, or a coloured full stop after it.**
4. **Eyebrows.** A tiny letter-spaced uppercase label sitting over a big heading. Labels
   are sentence case and earn their place, or they go.
5. **Monospace for decoration.** No mono kickers, counts or labels. Times use tabular
   figures in the same face.
6. **Decorative numbering** (01 / 02 / 03) on things already in order. Channel numbers
   are data, so they stay.

**Colour and surface**

7. **Purple, indigo or blue-violet gradients.** No gradient text and no gradient buttons.
8. **Cream or parchment backgrounds with terracotta orange.** This is the Claude palette,
   and the 2026 tell. Our darks are neutral and our whites are white.
9. **Glass.** No backdrop blur, and no translucent white borders on floating cards.
10. **Glows.** No coloured shadows, neon text-shadows or halos on focus.
11. **Blurry gradient blobs, aurora backgrounds or film grain as decoration.** The only
    gradients allowed are scrims that keep text readable over a picture.
12. **A soft shadow on every card.** Shadows only on things that float over the picture.

**Shape and components**

13. **Pill buttons and pill chips everywhere, or one big radius on everything.** Corners
    are small and square-ish. The only round things are dots and switch tracks.
14. **Generic thin-line icons next to every label, or emoji as icons.** Buttons say what
    they do, and the remote key is the hint.
15. **Identical cards that differ only by colour, or coloured tiles standing in for
    pictures.** Real posters come first. Drawn covers vary their layout and are set like
    a tape label, not a template.
16. **Symmetric triptychs, and everything centred in one column.** Layout follows the
    content: the guide is a grid, and reading text is left-aligned.

**Words**

17. **Em dashes in UI copy.**
18. **Weightless lines** like "Your X, reimagined", "Seamless", "Get started" or
    "Unlock". Copy says what is on the screen and what the button does.
19. **Placeholder names and numbers** that pass as real. Demo content says it is demo.

**Craft**

20. **Text that overflows or overlaps**, and states left unchecked. Every screen is
    screenshotted at phone, laptop and TV size, including empty, loading and error states.

## Where the look comes from instead

The subject is television, so the details come from television. We do not borrow from
startup landing pages.

- **Signal colour is tally red,** the light on a studio camera that means "you are on
  air". It is used only for now, live and progress.
- **Channel colours are the SMPTE colour bars:** yellow, cyan, green, magenta, red and
  blue at 75%.
- **The demo picture is a program slate:** the flat card a station runs before a tape,
  with the title, episode and running time.
- **Focus inverts to white,** like a highlighted row on a cable box, so it reads at 10
  feet.

## Sources

- Anthropic, [Improving frontend design through Skills](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills).
  The defaults it names are Inter, purple gradients on white, and minimal motion.
- [Claude Opus 5.5 gets better at frontend and UI design](https://promtexe.com/news/claude-opus-5-5-frontend-design-ai-look).
  It quotes Anthropic's prompting guide: cream backgrounds, italic accent words in
  headlines, numbered section labels, monospace labels and pill-shaped buttons.
- TeneX Studio, [AI slop: 8 signs a website was generated by AI](https://tenex.studio/en/blog/ai-slop-8-signs/).
  Its signs are em dashes, eyebrows, gradient headlines, oversized italic serif, no real
  images, decorative numbering, identical cards, and false numbers.
- 925 Studios, [AI slop fonts and gradients](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells).
  It covers Inter, indigo-500, the three-card row, thin-line icons, rounded cards with
  soft shadows, and weightless copy.
- [There are 3 telltale signs that you used AI to make your app](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/copilot/articles/3-telltale-signs-used-ai-040101406.html).
  It notes polish that outruns function, and missing empty, loading and error states.

What's left on purpose:

- The player bar keeps standard media icons (play, skip, volume, full screen). They are
  controls, not decoration.
- Small uppercase stays on broadcast badges like LIVE, HD and PG.
- Drawn covers are still placeholders. Real posters from TMDB or Kodi art replace them
  wherever a match is found.
